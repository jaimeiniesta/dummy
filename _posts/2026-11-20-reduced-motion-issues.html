Every case below gets prefers-reduced-motion wrong. Some of them change what is rendered depending on the setting, so scan this page with prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference and with prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and compare the results. Others ignore the setting completely; automated tools can't see those, but they are here for manual review.
